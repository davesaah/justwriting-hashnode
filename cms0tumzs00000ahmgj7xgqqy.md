The big should discourage the small.

Because it is not big, but small.

Comparisons are made between trees.

We are discouraged.

Everyone has a field. Whether it is full or empty.

Who should be excited? The forest or the tree?

I say both.

Because the field is full because it was empty.

When you see a forest, remember it was empty.

When you see an empty field, remember the forest.

Don't be distracted by a forest full of trees.

Tend to your field filled with seeds.
